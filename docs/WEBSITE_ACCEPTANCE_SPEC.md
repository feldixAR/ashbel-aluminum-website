# Website Acceptance Spec

## Purpose

This file defines what counts as an acceptable Ashbel Aluminum website. Do not treat the site as complete until these requirements are met.

## Global requirements

- Hebrew public content only.
- Full RTL alignment.
- Mobile first layout.
- Premium professional aluminum contractor positioning.
- Clear conversion paths on every relevant page.
- No fake login, dashboard, CRM, live pricing or automatic quote promises.
- No copied content from other websites.

## Required navigation

Primary navigation should be clear and not overloaded:

- ראשי
- שירותים
- מוצרים וסדרות
- פרויקטים
- איך מתקדמים
- אזור מקצוענים
- צור קשר

Optional secondary links may include styles, upload and about if the layout supports it without crowding.

Header must include a clear CTA: phone, WhatsApp or send plans.

Mobile menu must have large touch targets, clear grouping and visible CTA actions.

## Homepage acceptance

The homepage must include:

1. Premium hero
   - strong Hebrew H1
   - concise subheadline
   - two primary CTAs
   - trust points
   - architectural or aluminum inspired visual area

2. Services overview
   - 4 to 6 clear service cards
   - each card explains customer value, not only technical terms

3. Products and Kalil series preview
   - explain that systems are selected according to project needs
   - do not claim official authorization unless verified

4. Process section
   - plans or photos
   - professional review
   - measurement and coordination
   - production and installation
   - finishing and service

5. Professionals section
   - architects, contractors, supervisors and developers
   - send plans, specs, measurements or site photos

6. Projects preview
   - use truthful placeholders if real photos are missing
   - do not invent real project claims

7. Final CTA
   - phone, WhatsApp and send project details

The homepage must not look like a generic list of white cards.

## Internal page acceptance

Every internal page must include:

- page hero or intro section
- clear H1
- structured content sections
- CTA block where relevant
- metadata
- clean RTL layout
- mobile friendly spacing

Pages required:

- /services
- /styles
- /products
- /projects
- /process
- /professionals
- /upload
- /about
- /contact

## Contact and lead requirements

The site must clearly expose:

- phone number
- WhatsApp action
- contact form or fallback contact flow
- upload plans or photos flow
- professional consultation request

Use the business phone: 055-960-7033.

If backend upload is not implemented, present the flow honestly as sending plans, photos or details through available contact channels.

## SEO requirements

Each public page must have:

- unique title
- useful description
- one H1
- semantic structure
- natural Hebrew keywords
- meaningful internal links

Site level requirements:

- robots.txt
- sitemap.xml
- LocalBusiness JSON LD
- Open Graph basics
- meaningful alt text for images or visual placeholders

## UX and CRO requirements

- The first screen must make it clear what Ashbel Aluminum does and who it serves.
- CTAs must be visible and action oriented.
- Contact actions must not be hidden only in the footer.
- The site must support both private customers and professional users.
- The user should always know what to do next.

## Visual quality bar

The site should look like a finished premium business website, not a developer scaffold.

Reject the implementation if:

- most sections are flat white cards with weak hierarchy
- the header looks temporary
- mobile menu feels cramped
- page content looks dumped from data arrays
- CTAs are unclear
- internal pages look unfinished
