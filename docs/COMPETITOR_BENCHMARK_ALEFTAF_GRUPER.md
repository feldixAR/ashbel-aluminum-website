# Competitor Benchmark: Aleftaf and Gruper

## Scope
This benchmark is limited to two approved sources only:

1. Aleftaf, א.ת אלומיניום
2. Gruper, גרופר תעשיות אלומיניום

The B144 Gruper page is excluded.

## Purpose
Use these sites as a benchmark for completing the Ashbel Aluminum website. Do not copy them. Extract useful patterns for product presentation, project trust, brand hierarchy, typography, header/logo treatment, CTA structure and professional audience flow.

## Aleftaf patterns to adapt
Aleftaf is useful for authority, scale, project confidence and professional execution.

Adapt these ideas to Ashbel scale:

1. A stronger opening statement that sells confidence and result, not only services.
2. Project proof as a major credibility layer.
3. A clear story of experience, precision, innovation and professional service.
4. Operational confidence around materials, production, schedule and delivery.
5. Audience clarity for architects, entrepreneurs, supervisors, consultants and contractors.
6. Visual density that feels like a serious aluminum company rather than a generic landing page.

Do not copy large scale claims, customer logos, certifications, project numbers or industrial positioning that is not verified for Ashbel.

## Gruper patterns to adapt
Gruper is useful for product breadth and practical customer flow.

Adapt these ideas:

1. Products should be visible as categories, not only text cards.
2. Custom fit matters: measurements, colors, needs and site conditions.
3. Quote preparation begins with plans, specifications, openings list or photos.
4. Product families should be easy to understand for non technical customers.
5. Consultation is part of the flow, not an afterthought.

Do not copy product categories that Ashbel does not want to sell now. Do not claim national distribution, agency network or factory scale unless verified.

## Required implementation moves

1. Update `ASHBEL_WEBSITE_SPEC.md` with benchmark derived requirements.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Products page visually works as a premium category gallery.
2. Product categories are readable immediately.
3. Practical technical guidance remains below the gallery.
4. Project cards communicate context, challenge, solution and next material to send.
5. Header and logo feel more connected to aluminum/windows.
6. Upload/review path is visible as the main conversion path.
7. Desktop and mobile screenshots are inspected after the change.
8. No fake claims, fake photos or fake client names are added.
