# Competitor Benchmark: Aleftaf And Gruper

Approved reference sources only:

- Aleftaf, א.ת אלומיניום
- Gruper, גרופר תעשיות אלומיניום

Do not use the B144 Gruper page.

## Benchmark Lessons

- The site should feel like an aluminum/window company immediately, through frame, profile, glass and opening language.
- Product pages should be visual and category-led, with strong hierarchy and large product names.
- Trust should come from process clarity, professional intake, practical checks and truthful project framing.
- Avoid generic SaaS cards, weak placeholder sections and decorative visuals that do not communicate aluminum systems.
- Copy should stay professional, direct and Israeli Hebrew, without exaggerated claims.

## Ashbel Translation

- Use CSS-based architectural aluminum/glass visuals when real project assets are unavailable.
- Present products as categories and fit decisions, not as a dry list.
- Present projects as review scenarios until verified photos and proof are available.
- Make sending plans, photos, specifications and opening lists the main conversion action.
