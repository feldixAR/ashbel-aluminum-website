# Ashbel Website Product Visual Spec

## Approved Direction

- A.S. Aluminum inspiration: ordered product catalog, large product/category blocks, clear hierarchy, premium home/aluminum feeling, architect-friendly browsing.
- Gruper Aluminum inspiration: grounded Israeli field-company language, practical product/work categories, visible phone/WhatsApp/contact paths, relevance for homeowners, contractors and architects.
- Do not copy text, imagery, claims or proof from either reference.

## Simplified Public Navigation

- ראשי
- מוצרים ופתרונות
- דוגמאות מהשטח
- לאדריכלים וקבלנים
- העלאת תוכניות
- צור קשר

`/services`, `/styles`, `/process` and `/about` may remain available routes, but the public experience should not depend on them or repeat the main navigation.

## Merged Page Logic

- Products owns the main catalog experience.
- Projects is positioned as דוגמאות מהשטח, using realistic work scenarios rather than project proof.
- Upload owns the combined “איך מתקדמים” and “העלאת תוכניות ותיאום פגישה” flow.
- Professionals focuses on architects, designers, contractors, supervisors and developers.

## Copy Rules

- Hebrew only for public copy.
- Voice: practical Israeli aluminum company, not SaaS, consulting or QA language.
- Use concrete aluminum words: חלונות, ויטרינות, הזזה, תריסים, הצללה, המראה הבלגי, המראה המודרני, פתחים גדולים, שיפוצים, וילות, אדריכלים, קבלנים, מדידה, ייצור, התקנה נקייה.
- Do not expose defensive integrity language as marketing copy.
- Do not lead with “send photos and we will check what fits.”
- Do not present the first step as an automatic quote request or immediate price.
- Use meeting-oriented language: תוכנית אלומיניום, תוכנית אדריכלית, מידות, שאלה מקצועית, פגישה מקצועית, המשך טיפול מסודר.
- Photos are secondary, useful for small point questions or initial site context, not the main customer journey.
- Do not invent clients, certifications, addresses, maps, awards, partnerships, factory claims, real projects or real photos.

## Visual Direction

- Bright, premium and grounded: white, light aluminum, graphite, steel blue and useful contrast.
- Use CSS-based aluminum/glass/window visuals as decorative material feeling only.
- Avoid fake project photos, dark luxury, generic SaaS cards, repetitive equal sections and over-technical layouts.
- Product cards should be large, visual and category-led.

## Main Conversion Path

Primary conversion: תוכנית אלומיניום / תוכנית אדריכלית / מידות / שאלה מקצועית -> תיאום פגישה מקצועית -> הבנה עמוקה יותר -> הצעה בהמשך אם רלוונטי -> ביצוע.

WhatsApp, phone and contact fallback should support this path. The site must not imply an automatic quote or immediate price from photos.

## Final Readiness Gates

- The first screen explains who Ashbel is, what aluminum work it performs, who it serves and what to do next.
- The main action is meeting-oriented: העלאת תוכניות ותיאום פגישה.
- Upload is not a quote request page.
- Professionals is a professional entry point, not a fake login or user account.
- Products appear in this order: המראה הבלגי, המראה המודרני, ויטרינות והזזה, מערכות הצללה, פתרונות נוספים.
- Field examples feel practical without claiming completed projects.
- Mobile menu works, CTAs are visible early, and no horizontal overflow exists.
- Visual artifacts and E2E checks must pass before preview review.
